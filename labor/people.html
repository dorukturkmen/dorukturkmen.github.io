ASSOC. PROF. DR.

Pelin Aytemiz Karslı

Visual Culture, Memory, Vernacular Photography, Death, Everyday Life, Digital Media.

Dr. Pelin Aytemiz Karslı is an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Visual Communication Design in the Faculty of Communication at Yaşar University. She received her Ph.D. from Bilkent University with a dissertation titled Representing Absence and the Absent One: Remembering and Longing Through Mourning Photography. Her research focuses on visual culture, vernacular photography, photographic materiality, and digital media, with particular attention to memory, death, everyday life, and the intersections of tradition and modernity in Turkey. Alongside her academic work, she also produces short films and narrative projects. Her award-winning short film Forget Me Not (2019), together with her publications, reflects her ongoing interest in photography, visuality, memory, and loss. She is also engaged in archival culture, found photography, and collaborative social projects (such as; alternative education) in both academic and public spheres.

ASSOC. PROF. DR.

Ürün Yıldıran Önk

Television, TV Drama, OTT Platform, Genre Analysis, Audience Studies

Dr. Ürün Yıldıran Önk completed her M.A. (2004) and Ph.D. (2011) at Dokuz Eylül University’s Institute of Fine Arts, and was appointed Associate Professor in 2022. Since 2007, she has been a faculty member at Yaşar University’s Department of Radio, Television and Cinema, where she teaches at both undergraduate and graduate levels and currently holds the position of vice dean. Beyond her academic duties, she contributes to the coordinating team of the CONTACT International Student Film Festival and participates in various internationally and nationally funded projects. Her recent research focuses on television genres; notably, she coordinated ‘New Screen New Dizi,’ a project examining Turkish serials on OTT platforms. Her scholarship includes numerous articles, conference papers, and book chapters spanning television, cinema, and media history.

ASSOC. PROF. DR.

Burcu Dabak Özdemir

Turkish Cinema, gender, popular culture, and cultural identity.

Burcu Dabak Özdemir completed her PhD at the University of East Anglia with a dissertation titled Being Tourist in Geographies of Power. She holds master’s degrees from Kadir Has University and SOAS, University of London. In 2023–2024, she conducted postdoctoral research at HFF Potsdam-Babelsberg with a TÜBİTAK scholarship, focusing on Turkish film festivals in Europe. Dr. Özdemir is currently an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Communication, Yaşar University. She is the author of Cross-Dressing in Turkish Cinema: Politics, Gender and National Trauma (I.B. Tauris, 2021) and Experiencing National Culture Abroad: Turkish Film Festivals in Germany (Palgrave Macmillan, 2024). Her award-winning documentaries, including Yerin Ruhu (2023) and Yersiz Yurtsuz (2009), alongside numerous scholarly articles, explore themes of memory, space, gender, and cultural identity.

ASST. PROF. DR.

Doruk Türkmen

Cultural Heritage, Game-based Learning, Research through Design, Game Design, Meaning-making

Doruk Türkmen is a designer and academic whose work focuses on the intersection of interaction, play, and meaning. He currently serves as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Visual Communication Design at Yaşar University, İzmir. His research investigates emergent meaning in interactive systems, with a particular emphasis on games and playful artifacts as sites of experiential and cultural production. Adopting a research-through-design approach, Türkmen explores how interaction and play can generate new forms of understanding, engagement, and interpretation. His doctoral research, conducted in the Design Studies Program at İzmir University of Economics, centers on the design of role-playing games within the context of archaeology museums, examining how digital and hybrid game experiences can enrich museum engagement by mediating cultural heritage through meaningful play. Through this work, he contributes to interdisciplinary discussions spanning design research, game studies, and museum studies, while also developing experimental prototypes that bridge theory and practice.

ASSOC. PROF. DR.

Tülay Görü Doğan

AI literacy, media and communication education, digital learning environments, everyday digital practices.

Tulay GORU DOGAN is a faculty member in the Department of New Media and Communication at Akdeniz University, Faculty of Communication. She holds a PhD in Distance Education and an MA in Visual Communication Design from Anadolu University. Her research focuses on artificial intelligence literacy, media education, and digital learning environments. She is interested in how digital technologies, particularly AI and platform-based systems, are increasingly embedded in everyday life and shape communication practices and learning experiences. Her earlier work explored the use of infographics in learning and the role of social media in education. More recently, her research has expanded to include user experience (UX) design, computational thinking, and AI-supported learning environments. Her academic interests include educational technologies, AI in education, and the design of innovative learning and communication systems.

ASST. PROF. DR.

Gizem Kızıltunalı Sonyol

Critical cultural communication, visual culture, gender, fashion, ideology, symbolic regimes, representation.

Gizem Kızıltunalı Sonyol is an Assistant Professor in the Department of New Media and Communication at Yaşar University, Türkiye. She holds a PhD in Media from Manchester Metropolitan University, where she also taught for four years. Her research brings together critical cultural communication, visual culture, and media studies to examine how power operates through images, materiality, and everyday cultural forms. She is particularly interested in the politics of gender, identity, ideology, and cultural memory, and in the ways visual and symbolic regimes shape visibility, legitimacy, and value across media, fashion, and material culture. Across her work, she explores how cultural meanings are negotiated, authorized, and transformed, and who has the power to determine what they mean. Her research pays particular attention to the relationship between representation and symbolic authority, asking how images and objects become invested with social meaning, how aesthetic arrangements naturalize hierarchy, and how acts of interpretation are bound up with broader struggles over recognition, value, and cultural legitimacy.

INSTRUCTOR DR.

Simge Gökbayrak

Digital Storytelling, New Media Practices, Posthumanism, Cyborg Art, Creative Media Production, Technology–Society Relations, Everyday Life Practices

Dr. Simge Gökbayrak is a faculty member in the Department of New Media and Communication at Yaşar University, where she has been teaching since 2019 and working full-time since 2022. She received her Ph.D. in Cinema and Television from Anadolu University with a dissertation titled *The Cyborgized Human as an Inevitable Social Reality and Cyborg Art*. Her research focuses on digital storytelling, new media practices, posthumanism, cyborg art, and technology–society relations, adopting an interdisciplinary and critical perspective. Alongside her academic work, Gökbayrak has extensive experience in the creative industries, having worked as an assistant director and director in advertising, cinema, television, and music video productions since 2005. She has contributed to nearly 200 projects and continues to bridge academic knowledge with practice-based production. Her short films İz (2011) and ID (2008), for which she served as director, producer, and screenwriter, have been screened and awarded at numerous national and international festivals, including selections at Cannes and Berlinale. In addition to her teaching and research, she actively contributes to university–industry collaborations, serves as a jury member at film festivals, and has been a board member and industry advisor at EkoFilm – Sustainable Production Platform since 2024.
