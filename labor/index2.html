Tracking the Everyday,
Shaping the Future.

LAB-OR (Lab of the Ordinary) is an interdisciplinary research initiative dedicated to exploring the politics of everyday life through the lenses of media, technology, space, and affective practices. By bridging academic production with artistic and public projects, the lab investigates the micro-forms of resistance and solidarity embedded within the ordinary.
